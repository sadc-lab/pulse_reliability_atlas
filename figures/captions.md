# Figure captions

Captions for figures generated by `scripts/make_figures.py`. Each caption
references the analysis artifact and the experiment log entry that
produced it.

---

## Figure 1 — Per-cell flag breakdown

`fig01_flag_summary.{png,pdf}` — source: `results/cell_flag_summary.csv`,
script: `scripts/make_figures.py::fig_flag_summary`, log: S006.

Stacked bars of run counts per flag, faceted by cell (scenario × sex). The
8 cells of the full Morris sweep each contain N = 160 runs from the same
canonical Morris sample. *Clean* (green) runs are the input to the Morris
sensitivity analysis (Figures 2 a–f and 3); the four *flagged* categories
characterize where Pulse fails to produce a usable steady-state output.
The clear gradient from S1 → S4 in the *not_steady_state* and
*implausible* categories is itself the primary "operational envelope" map
that the paper claims as its first deliverable.

## Figures 2 a–f — Morris μ\* heatmaps per output

`fig02_morris_mu_star_<output>.{png,pdf}` — source:
`results/morris_indices.parquet`, script:
`scripts/make_figures.py::fig_morris_mu_star`, log: S006.

For each of the six outputs (HR, MAP, Vt, SpO2, PaO2, PaCO2), one heatmap
shows μ\* for every input × cell, with cells on the y-axis (S1_M …
S4_F) and inputs on the x-axis. The dominant diagonal pattern (each
output dominated by 1–3 inputs) is the visual instantiation of H1.
Bootstrap 95 % CIs on each μ\* are recorded in the underlying parquet
(`mu_star_lo` and `mu_star_hi` columns) but elided from the heatmap
for legibility.

## Figure 3 — H1 evaluation

`fig03_h1_top3_fraction.{png,pdf}` — source:
`results/h1_top3_fraction.csv`, script:
`scripts/make_figures.py::fig_h1_evaluation`, log: S006.

Heatmap of the top-3 fraction of summed μ\* per cell × output. The
preregistered threshold is 0.5 (red ↔ green crossover at 0.5 in the
RdYlGn palette). Every cell × output pair above 0.5 supports H1; the
median fraction across the 48 cells is reported alongside the figure in
the manuscript.

## Figure 4 — H4 Wasserstein distance, M vs F

`fig04_h4_wasserstein.{png,pdf}` — source:
`results/wasserstein_male_vs_female.csv`, script:
`scripts/make_figures.py::fig_h4_wasserstein`, log: S006 / S008.

Per scenario × output, the 1-Wasserstein distance between the male
and female clean-run distributions, in the output's native units
(bpm, mmHg, mL, %, mmHg, mmHg). Each observed distance is compared
against a within-sex sub-sampling null (n_null = 2,000 random splits
of the combined M+F clean runs into halves of the original sizes);
distances marked with `*` exceed the 95th percentile of the null.
Under Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control at q = 0.05, **5 of 24** (cell ×
output) combinations survive the within-sex null — all 5 on
gas-exchange channels (PaCO2 × 3, SpO2 × 1, PaO2 × 1). The
naive-α = 0.05 analysis would flag 6; S2 SpO2 (p_emp = 0.022 above
the 0.0104 BH critical) is the BH-demoted row. Cardiovascular and
Vt distances do not reach significance even without correction.

## Figure 5 — H4 Spearman ρ on μ\* rankings

`fig05_h4_spearman_mu_star.{png,pdf}` — source:
`results/spearman_mu_star.csv`, script:
`scripts/make_figures.py::fig_h4_spearman`, log: S006 / S008.

Per scenario × output, Spearman correlation between the male and
female Morris μ\* vectors (each indexed by input), with paired-input
bootstrap 95 % CI. ρ = 1 ⇒ identical input ranking between sexes;
ρ = −1 ⇒ inverted ranking. Computed only for scenarios S2 and S3;
S1 and S4_F do not have a computable ρ because FiO2 has zero clean
elementary-effect pairs in those cells (every Morris OAT step that
flips FiO2 between 0.21 and 1.0 has at least one endpoint flagged
by the SpO2 ceiling pinning at FiO2 ≈ 1.0; this is itself a finding,
discussed in §4.3 limitations). Point estimates are 0.75 – 1.00 in
S2 / S3, with bootstrap CI lower bounds ≥ 0.34 for most outputs;
Vt is the exception (S2 CI [−0.06, 1.0]), reflecting that the
ranking of inputs driving Vt is not clearly preserved across sex.
Rankings are qualitatively preserved but the per-output precision
is modest because the ρ vector has only 7 entries.

## Figure 6 — Reliability traffic light

`fig06_reliability_traffic_light.{png,pdf}` — source:
`results/reliability_classification.csv`, script:
`scripts/make_figures.py::fig_reliability_traffic_light`, log: S006.

User-facing summary. Per cell × output, one of {green, yellow, red}:

* **green** — no flags raised in the cell AND clean-run mean within 10 %
  of the (S1, M) baseline;
* **yellow** — any flag raised OR mean 10–30 % off baseline;
* **red** — mean > 30 % off baseline OR diverged + implausible rate
  > 30 %.

This is the figure most likely to be consulted in practice by Pulse
users; the paper's discussion section anchors recommendations to this
grid.
